# Sources and data contracts

Snapshot date: 9 November 2026. This is an independent fan project, not affiliated with Jagex, RuneLite, GroupIron, or the OSRS Wiki. RuneScape and game artwork are © Jagex.

- Equipment names, exact variants, slots, bonuses and attack speeds: [OSRS Wiki calculator equipment data](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/cdn/json/equipment.json). NPC combat stats: [monster data](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/cdn/json/monsters.json). Wiki-derived factual data and sprites require attribution to the OSRS Wiki contributors and Jagex as applicable. See the upstream [license](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/LICENSE) and [Wiki licensing](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape:Copyrights). We do not redistribute the DPS calculation engine or claim its validation.
- Item sprites: `https://static.runelite.net/cache/item/icon/{item_id}.png`, cached locally for supported items. Game artwork © Jagex.
- Classic site textures and Kingthings heading font: the public [OSRS website](https://oldschool.runescape.com/) assets at `https://www.runescape.com/img/rsp777/bg2.jpg`, `scroll/backdrop_745.gif`, `scroll/backdrop_765_top.gif`, `scroll/backdrop_765_bottom.gif`, and `https://www.runescape.com/img/global/fonts/Kingthings_Petrock-webfont.woff`. Used for the requested 2007-era fan-site style; original rights remain with their respective owners.
- RuneScape font: [GroupIron's bundled font](https://github.com/christoabrown/group-ironmen/tree/master/site/public/fonts), used for the requested game style. GroupIron source is BSD-2-Clause; game/font rights remain with their respective owners.
- Legacy snapshot skill/quest serialization originated in GroupIron’s public schema. It is retained for backup compatibility; there is no GroupIron connection and quest data is not used for eligibility or exports.
- Public level lookup: [Wise Old Man player endpoint](https://docs.wiseoldman.net/api/players/player-endpoints). Only the requested name is sent; no bank or credentials are transmitted. Official hiscores JSON is also accepted by the native private-check tool.
- Skill requirements: [OSRSReboxed public item data](https://github.com/0xNeffarion/osrsreboxed-db), GPL-3.0, joined to the Wiki equipment catalog. These manually maintained requirements are supplemented by reviewed overrides. Missing requirement data remains distinct from a known empty requirement list.
- Combat formulas and reference tests: OSRS Wiki calculator [PlayerVsNPCCalc](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/lib/PlayerVsNPCCalc.ts), [BaseCalc](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/lib/BaseCalc.ts), [Equipment](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/lib/Equipment.ts), [SpellMax](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/types/SpellMax.ts), [ToA scaling](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/lib/scaling/TombsOfAmascut.ts), and [BasicRolls regression examples](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/tests/calc/BasicRolls.test.ts). Rust independently implements the supported calculations; it does not embed the Wiki TypeScript engine or inherit its full validation coverage.
- Bank Tags CSV: RuneLite’s [TabInterface export/import implementation](https://github.com/runelite/runelite/blob/master/runelite-client/src/main/java/net/runelite/client/plugins/banktags/tabs/TabInterface.java). The v1 CSV contains a name, icon and item IDs; quantities remain an Inventory Setups concern.
