# Sources and data contracts

Snapshot date: 9 November 2026. This is an independent fan project, not affiliated with Jagex, RuneLite, GroupIron, or the OSRS Wiki. RuneScape and game artwork are © Jagex.

- Equipment names/slots: [OSRS Wiki calculator equipment data](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/cdn/json/equipment.json). Wiki-derived factual data and sprites require attribution to the OSRS Wiki contributors and Jagex as applicable. See the upstream [license](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/LICENSE) and [Wiki licensing](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape:Copyrights). We do not redistribute the DPS calculation engine or claim its validation.
- Item sprites: `https://static.runelite.net/cache/item/icon/{item_id}.png`, cached locally for supported items. Game artwork © Jagex.
- RuneScape font: [GroupIron's bundled font](https://github.com/christoabrown/group-ironmen/tree/master/site/public/fonts), used for the requested game style. GroupIron source is BSD-2-Clause; game/font rights remain with their respective owners.
- GroupIron [member schema](https://github.com/christoabrown/group-ironmen/blob/master/server/src/models.rs), [read endpoint](https://github.com/christoabrown/group-ironmen/blob/master/server/src/authed.rs), [skill order](https://github.com/christoabrown/group-ironmen/blob/master/site/src/data/skill.js), [quest mapping](https://github.com/christoabrown/group-ironmen/blob/master/site/src/data/quest.js), and [quest data](https://github.com/christoabrown/group-ironmen/blob/master/site/public/data/quest_data.json). The bundled map sorts non-hidden quest IDs numerically; state 0=in progress, 1=not started, 2=finished. Skills are XP arrays in alphabetical order, excluding Overall, with Sailing appended. A member timestamp does not prove per-container freshness.
- [Barrows strategy](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Barrows/Strategies) and [Barrows access](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Barrows): Priest in Peril and starting His Faithful Servants are separated from suggested combat levels. Wind spells supply a simple supported magic route. Air staves supply air runes; a 150-cast rune allowance is a trip-template choice, not an observed requirement.
- [ToA strategy](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Tombs_of_Amascut/Strategies): Beneath Cursed Sands access, three-style equipment, Keris weapon utility, melee core work and pickaxe utility. The authored 0–150 model is packing guidance, not combat simulation or a complete invocation guide.
- Progression: [Piety](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Piety), [Super restore](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Super_restore), [Trident of the seas](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Trident_of_the_seas). Meeting a skill threshold does not prove ownership or prayer unlocks.
- Inventory Setups [portable wrapper](https://github.com/dillydill123/inventory-setups/blob/master/src/main/java/inventorysetups/serialization/InventorySetupPortable.java), [setup model](https://github.com/dillydill123/inventory-setups/blob/master/src/main/java/inventorysetups/serialization/InventorySetupSerializable.java), [item model](https://github.com/dillydill123/inventory-setups/blob/master/src/main/java/inventorysetups/serialization/InventorySetupItemSerializable.java), and [import entry point](https://github.com/dillydill123/inventory-setups/blob/master/src/main/java/inventorysetups/InventorySetupsPlugin.java). The application writes its own Rust encoder matching those interfaces; no game actions are sent.

The starter priority lists and readiness cutoffs are authored heuristics. They do not establish a mathematically optimal setup, expected completion time, or a player's ability to complete an encounter. See README for intentionally unsupported mechanics.
