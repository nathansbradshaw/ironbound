# Sources and data contracts

Snapshot date: 9 November 2026. This is an independent fan project, not affiliated with Jagex, RuneLite, GroupIron, or the OSRS Wiki. RuneScape and game artwork are © Jagex.

- Inventory food restoration: RuneLite's public [ItemStatChanges](https://github.com/runelite/runelite/blob/master/runelite-client/src/main/java/net/runelite/client/plugins/itemstats/ItemStatChanges.java), [item IDs](https://github.com/runelite/runelite/blob/master/runelite-api/src/main/java/net/runelite/api/gameval/ItemID.java), and [Anglerfish scaling](https://github.com/runelite/runelite/blob/master/runelite-client/src/main/java/net/runelite/client/plugins/itemstats/food/Anglerfish.java). Rust imports factual food profiles; it does not execute or embed the Java implementation. Food sorting uses potential HP per slot, including remaining bites and separately labelled delayed healing. Potion restoration uses the existing ordinary Prayer model; healing and reviewed boosts are compared within their purpose. Boost doses do not stack. Unknown effects remain explicitly unranked. RuneLite is BSD-2-Clause; see its [license](https://github.com/runelite/runelite/blob/master/LICENSE).

- Equipment weight in kilograms: [OSRSReboxed item data](https://github.com/0xNeffarion/osrsreboxed-db/blob/master/docs/items-complete.json), GPL-3.0. The Rust `site item-weights /path/to/items-complete.json` command joins exact, unnoted IDs to the equipment catalog. This snapshot has weights for 4,686 of 5,096 equipment IDs; missing/newer variants remain unknown, never weightless. Setup mass includes equipped items, not packed supplies or run-energy mechanics.
- Recommended gear roles: [ToA strategy](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Tombs_of_Amascut/Strategies) and [Moons strategy](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Moons_of_Peril/Strategies). `assets/encounter-guidance.json` holds reviewed owned-item progression tiers and weapon prerequisites. These are starting roles, not a universal BIS table. The default uses them with supported attack windows; unknown DPS preserves the seed instead of ranking by defence alone. The planner allows up to 3% extra modelled attack time for reviewed role/prayer/defence tie-breaks; fully modelled normal attacks use a 15% band and expected damage, subtracting verified boss healing from outgoing damage. These tolerances are explicit planner policy, not Wiki formulas. Specials, supplies and full raid survival remain partial.
- The constrained search keeps hard pairwise switch, shared stock and bag limits. Recommended compares supported attack time; verified normal-attack profiles compare incoming damage within a 15% time band. Search is bounded, not a proven global optimum. Custom weights remain an explicit alternative: DPS, selected defence-bonus, prayer and equipped mass use fixed ideal-set relative utility, with normalization offsets; these are not damage prevention predictions. Old non-default custom weights remain custom; untouched legacy defaults become Recommended.
- Equipment names, exact variants, slots, bonuses and attack speeds: [OSRS Wiki calculator equipment data](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/cdn/json/equipment.json). NPC combat stats: [monster data](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/cdn/json/monsters.json). Wiki-derived factual data and sprites require attribution to the OSRS Wiki contributors and Jagex as applicable. See the upstream [license](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/LICENSE) and [Wiki licensing](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/RuneScape:Copyrights). We do not redistribute the DPS calculation engine or claim its validation.
- Boss/mode names: RuneLite's public [HiscoreSkill catalog](https://github.com/runelite/runelite/blob/master/runelite-client/src/main/java/net/runelite/client/hiscore/HiscoreSkill.java), 72 boss/mode entries in this snapshot. The Rust `site catalog-bosses` tool imports the names. Group templates add raid rooms and wave enemy types; their presence does not establish complete combat support.
- Mechanic coverage and raid/phase IDs: the Wiki calculator's public [constants](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/lib/constants.ts) and monster data. Unknown scaling, immunities and phase rules explicitly withhold DPS. NPCs sharing an ID may have different calculator variants; the compact catalog prefers regular/post-quest attack windows and is not an exhaustive version selector.
- Moons mechanics: [Moons of Peril strategy](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Moons_of_Peril/Strategies), [Blood Moon](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Blood_Moon), and the public NPC data above. The implemented profile covers normal dependent three-hit chains, flat armour and normal Blood healing; it does not inherit the Wiki's complete fight strategy or simulate special phases.
- Prayer drain and duration: [Prayer drain mechanics](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Prayer#Prayer_drain), [prayer bonuses](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Prayer_items), and supported boost definitions cross-checked against the Wiki calculator's [Prayer data](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/enums/Prayer.ts). Continuous drain and ordinary restoration estimates exclude encounter drains, flicking and restoration modifiers. Prayer unlocks are user-selected; only skill requirements are checked.
- Item sprites and names: RuneLite's public [static cache snapshot](https://github.com/runelite/static.runelite.net/tree/gh-pages/cache/item). `cargo run --bin site -- assets-inventory` downloads one public archive, validates the PNGs, and bundles every positive named item ID's icon. The browser receives the same complete icon pack; it makes no bank-derived image requests. Static builds copy only assets referenced by the HTML/CSS, keeping duplicate icon files and embedded source catalogs out of the deployment. Game artwork © Jagex.
- Classic site textures and Kingthings heading font: the public [OSRS website](https://oldschool.runescape.com/) assets at `https://www.runescape.com/img/rsp777/bg2.jpg`, `scroll/backdrop_745.gif`, `scroll/backdrop_765_top.gif`, `scroll/backdrop_765_bottom.gif`, and `https://www.runescape.com/img/global/fonts/Kingthings_Petrock-webfont.woff`. Used for the requested 2007-era fan-site style; original rights remain with their respective owners.
- RuneScape font: [GroupIron's bundled font](https://github.com/christoabrown/group-ironmen/tree/master/site/public/fonts), used for the requested game style. GroupIron source is BSD-2-Clause; game/font rights remain with their respective owners.
- Legacy snapshot skill/quest serialization originated in GroupIron’s public schema. It is retained for backup compatibility; there is no GroupIron connection and quest data is not used for eligibility or exports.
- Public level lookup: [Wise Old Man player endpoint](https://docs.wiseoldman.net/api/players/player-endpoints). Only the requested name is sent; no bank or credentials are transmitted. Official hiscores JSON is also accepted by the native private-check tool.
- Skill requirements: [OSRSReboxed public item data](https://github.com/0xNeffarion/osrsreboxed-db), GPL-3.0, joined to the Wiki equipment catalog. These manually maintained requirements are supplemented by reviewed overrides. Missing requirement data remains distinct from a known empty requirement list.
- Combat formulas and reference tests: OSRS Wiki calculator [PlayerVsNPCCalc](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/lib/PlayerVsNPCCalc.ts), [BaseCalc](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/lib/BaseCalc.ts), [Equipment](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/lib/Equipment.ts), [SpellMax](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/types/SpellMax.ts), [ToA scaling](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/lib/scaling/TombsOfAmascut.ts), and [BasicRolls regression examples](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/tests/calc/BasicRolls.test.ts). Rust independently implements the supported calculations; it does not embed the Wiki TypeScript engine or inherit its full validation coverage.
- Bank Tags CSV: RuneLite’s [TabInterface export/import implementation](https://github.com/runelite/runelite/blob/master/runelite-client/src/main/java/net/runelite/client/plugins/banktags/tabs/TabInterface.java). The v1 CSV contains a name, icon and item IDs; quantities remain an Inventory Setups concern.
- Raid room selection: [Chambers of Xeric](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Chambers_of_Xeric) and [The Fractured Archive strategy](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/The_Fractured_Archive/Strategies), checked 9 November 2026. Archive routes require the Guardian and Ocellus, plus one room from each fork: Ezekiel/Bork and the Smorks, Moia/Dreadnoth, Xarth/Dagon'hai. Bundled public NPC IDs supply target windows. `assets/raid-routes.json` owns room membership and defaults; encounter group defaults derive from it. Starting styles are editable suggestions and do not establish complete phase, immunity, add or scaling support. Archive DPS is withheld throughout.

- Crystal bow/Bowfa mechanics: [Wiki calculator](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/lib/PlayerVsNPCCalc.ts), independent piece contributions (helm1/legs2/body3), accuracy +5% and damage +2.5% per contribution, with damage flooring; only active exact variants. [Bowfa](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Bow_of_Faerdhinen) requires80Ranged70Agility; [crystal body](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Crystal_body)70Defence50Agility. Reviewed family-level equipment requirements supplement incomplete catalog data for [Moon equipment](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Moon_equipment) and [Avernic treads](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Avernic_treads). No quest checks.
- Interface artwork: unmodified `297-0.png`, `170-0.png`, `inventory_background.png`, `border.png`, `border-tiny.png`, `border-button.png` and the Chat-07 regular/bold and tiny fonts from [GroupIron’s original asset bundle](https://github.com/christoabrown/group-ironmen/tree/25a5e57704a3186582d3dd24ca081d8b1e492125/site/public). Source revision: `25a5e57704a3186582d3dd24ca081d8b1e492125`. Sprite slicing and equipment/inventory proportions follow that source. These are original assets, not generated replacements. Runtime and source distributions include `assets/osrs/NOTICE.txt` with the BSD-2-Clause notice; original game artwork/font rights remain with Jagex.

- Boss feedback fixes (12 November 2026): [Vorkath strategy](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Vorkath/Strategies), [Wiki calculator attack formulas](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/lib/PlayerVsNPCCalc.ts), [accuracy rolls](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/lib/BaseCalc.ts), and [ammunition capabilities](https://github.com/weirdgloop/osrs-dps-calc/blob/main/src/lib/Equipment.ts). Reviewed rules now include DHCB dragon bonuses, correct Salve variants, complete Void/Elite Void sets, and exact normal/dragon crossbow ammunition. Ruby and diamond enchantments are calculated; other unmodelled enchantments are identified as physically compatible while DPS is withheld. Whole owned-loadout comparison runs when selecting a reviewed single boss or explicitly comparing styles; manual style edits remain available.
- [Confliction gauntlets](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Confliction_gauntlets): the one-handed PvM passive uses the stationary same-target/same-spell miss-to-reroll process with the shared defence-roll reroll formula. It composes with the Brimstone ring. This is sustained damage, excluding the first-cast transient and secondary AoE targets; the passive does not apply to two-handed weapons. The actual wear requirement is 90 Hitpoints.
- [Yama](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Yama), [strategy](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Yama/Strategies) and [Judge](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Judge_of_Yama): generic window metadata distinguishes main-target and off-tank magic defence, final-phase stats, source NPC IDs, and phase progress budgets. Internal window keys are not game NPC IDs. Main boss progress is split 1,667/833 HP; the two Judge packing windows represent 400 HP per style across both interludes. Judge/contract outgoing DPS remains withheld; movement, glyphs, protection switching and full incoming damage are not simulated. Arclight/Emberlight use generic demonbane vulnerability scaling. Purging staff/Mark of Darkness remain unsupported.
