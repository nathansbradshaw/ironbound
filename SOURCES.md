# Sources and data contracts

- Nex/raid outgoing and readiness (13 November 2026): [assumptions, pinned primary sources, formulas and refresh commands](docs/raid-support.md). Adds explicit Nex phase/protection/mage states, Chambers party/CM scaling and pickaxes, Theatre normal/hard party HP, Nylocas colours and raid-loaned Dawnbringer, Archive recorded-state HP probes, and solo-300 Expert ToA. These provide scoped outgoing damage and gear screens; full-fight timing, team contribution, incoming mechanics and supplies remain separate.

- Energy recovery and Lightbearer (13 November 2026): [item mechanics and April 2024 equip fix](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Lightbearer), [special energy](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Special_attack), [game ticks](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Game_tick), and pinned RuneLite [`bb0fa42` regen meter](https://github.com/runelite/runelite/blob/bb0fa42b3d9329ffe31115f1f20042c455def2e3/runelite-client/src/main/java/net/runelite/client/plugins/regenmeter/RegenMeterPlugin.java). Natural recovery is 10% per 50 ticks, or 25 with the worn ring; full energy pauses the timer. Equipping preserves a pulse within 25 ticks; unequipping resets 50. RuneLite is client overlay corroboration, not server code or gameplay verification. [Bounded rotation contract and remaining gaps](docs/energy-rotations.md).

- Zorya / Crimson kisten (13 November 2026): [special-attack audit, primary sources and limits](docs/zorya-kisten.md). Zorya's opening modifiers and kisten's binomial distribution follow pinned calculator revision 74958b1. The Rust follow-up state machine extends that calculator's partial Zorya support using the official/Wiki buff description, stopping at target death and preserving explicitly held empowerment. Accurate-zero triggers and cooldown sums remain source/formula assumptions, not new gameplay measurements. Kisten follows the current 130–170% four-success band; the announced but reference-observed absent final minus one is documented. Normal gear scores and full-fight simulations do not consume these special estimates.

- Leafy Magic route: [Slayer's staff](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Slayer%27s_staff) and [enchanted staff](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Slayer%27s_staff_(e)) define 50/75 Magic and 55 Slayer, one death/four mind runes, normal `10 + floor(Magic/10)` damage, and the enchanted `13 + floor(Magic/6)` only on an explicitly marked eligible Slayer task. Spell damage then uses the ordinary equipment/task stages. These two staffs now cast Magic Dart; other staff variants require their own reviewed spell compatibility. Turoth/Kurask bestiary gear screens consider owned usable leaf-bladed weapons, compatible broad ammo and these staffs. Their 55/70 Slayer entry checks remain separate.

- [Mad Angel](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Mad_Angel) counters affect only the first splat: cleave forces accuracy and clamps damage to half the complete attack maximum (half the first maximum for chained macuahuitl); perfect lightning forces accuracy and maximises only that splat. Follow-ups retain normal accuracy. The pinned calculator supplies the exact minimum-clamp and split-hit behavior; this does not assert counter frequency. Verac bypass and full Blood Moon cooldown interactions are gated. [Maggot strategy](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Maggot_King/Strategies) and pinned Far/Nearby/Roaring variants distinguish melee reach, +150/+400 Magic defence and 200/100 Defence. The reviewed **crush** punish applies a 15% defence roll and 3/2 rolled damage only to the first splat. Other punish styles, stacking fire-spell burn/defence changes, free-attack rate and incoming attacks remain unverified. Ordinary ranged/melee readiness assumes no punish bonus.
- [Shellbane strategy](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Shellbane_gryphon/Strategies) and [Slayer boss requirements](https://oldschool.runescape.wiki/w/Slayer_boss): the post-quest ordinary target retains air weakness; a usable owned tortugan shield is equipped in the **cape slot** for its melee gear screen.
