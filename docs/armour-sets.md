# Armour set comparisons

The planner compares ordinary armour with joint equipment candidates. It refines each candidate's remaining slots, then uses the selected Recommended policy or custom weights. Owned stock, usable variants, levels, exclusions, pinned slots, ranged category and later switch/inventory limits still apply. This bounded search is not proof of global BIS.

| Equipment | Calculated and compared | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Void / Elite Void | Complete melee, ranged and magic sets, including mixed normal/elite pieces. | Matching helm and every required slot must be present. |
| Crystal | Crystal bow/Bowfa jointly with every owned armour subset. Each piece retains its own accuracy/damage contribution. | Active variants only. A pinned non-crystal helmet can remain; other weapons receive no crystal bonus. |
| Dharok | Complete armour **and greataxe**, with per-roll missing-health scaling. | Combat → Boosts, darts & debuffs → **Current Hitpoints (Dharok / Torag)**. Blank means full health. This is a fixed health assumption, not a prediction of survival or healing. |
| Verac | Complete armour/flail, with a 25% guaranteed hit rolling 1 through base maximum + 1. Full set plus Amulet of the Damned also adds 7 Prayer. | Unsupported NPC protection/immunity scenarios remain gated. |
| Guthan | Complete armour/warspear and 25% potential healing. | Displayed recovery precedes overheal and remaining-target-HP caps. It is not subtracted from incoming damage or credited as guaranteed trip sustain. |
| Karil | Complete armour/crossbow, exact bolt racks and Amulet of the Damned's 25% extra half-damage hitsplat. | Run-energy/Agility debuffs are not PvM damage bonuses. |
| Ahrim | Complete robes/staff and Amulet of the Damned's 25% chance of 30% extra spell damage, with per-roll flooring. Full set plus amulet also permits Ice spells. | Dynamic NPC Strength drains remain outside the model. |
| Torag | Complete armour/hammers and two independently rolled hits, with NPC flat armour applied to each. Amulet of the Damned increases effective Defence using rounded missing-health percentage, after prayer and before magic-defence weighting. | Run-energy drain has no PvM damage bonus. Current Hitpoints is a fixed assumption. |
| Obsidian | Armour, compatible melee weapon and optional Berserker necklace jointly. Armour adds a floored 10% of the base roll/hit after Salve/task; necklace scales each damage roll by 20%. | Cape/shield do not complete the armour set. Other weapons receive no Obsidian bonus. |
| Inquisitor | All owned piece subsets; current crush multipliers are 0.5% helm, 1% body, 1% legs. | Crush only; the removed mace-specific multiplier is not applied. |
| Justiciar | Complete armour candidates for melee, ranged and magic. Incoming typed damage is reduced per roll using the matching equipment Defence bonus / 3000, with at least 1 damage removed from positive hits. | No reduction to typeless Moon chains; negative Magic defence never increases damage. Unknown encounter specials remain gated. Defensive ranking still requires a verified attack-frequency profile. |
| Blood Moon | Complete armour and dual macuahuitl. Two separately rolled damage halves; the second accuracy check requires the first to succeed. Bloodrager rolls exactly 33% per accurate splat to shorten the next attack by one tick. | Mean cooldown is used in sustained DPS and supported finite-HP budgets. Accurate zero-damage hits retain eligibility. Unverified guaranteed-hit/special-phase transforms and special attacks remain gated. |
| Eclipse Moon | Complete armour and atlatl, with actual dart stock. Ranged accuracy/prayers, Strength level and melee Strength equipment bonuses; melee Salve/task damage modifiers. Finite-HP timing uses actual joint hit/burn outcomes, a target-local four-tick pulse clock, ten pulses per stack, five-stack blocking and imported immunities. | 4,096 seeded trials report mean attacks, time to the HP boundary, committed cooldown and approximate 95% sampling error. Each entity/window starts empty. Full-fight phase clearing is explicit and configurable; retained stacks age without damage during immune downtime. Unverified special-window transforms remain gated. |
| Blue Moon | Complete armour and spear for both spellcasting and melee. Standard binding, Ice and Grasp spell recipes, exact rune costs, independent melee rolls after accurate spell hits (20% for binding/Ice, 50% for Grasp). | Set **Blue Moon free melee** under combat assumptions to Stab, Slash or Crush, enable automatic melee in game, and stay in melee range. Default Off counts spells only. Freeze immunity does not suppress a proc after an accurate cast. No special attack, AoE or multi-target damage is credited. |
| Virtus | Every owned armour subset; the existing 2% damage per piece gains another 3% for supported Ice spells. Ancient staff and full Ahrim/amulet casting are supported alongside Blue Moon. | Single-target Ice damage only; other Ancient spells and their secondary effects are outside the supported spell catalogue. Requires 78 Magic and 75 Defence. |

Stat-only combinations such as Bandos, Torva, Armadyl and Masori use their individual item stats.

Barrows active degradation variants can mix within one brother's set. Broken pieces and another brother's pieces cannot complete it. Moon armour can complete its set even when broken, using its zero equipment stats and actual level requirements. Recoil and dynamic NPC stat drains are not added to sustained player DPS. Health and Blue Moon assumptions travel with shared setups and export notes.

`tests/armour_sets.rs` checks independently derived per-roll arithmetic, joint discovery, complete-set requirements, finite-HP miss loops, guaranteed-max windows, per-hitsplat acid/armour rounding, and setup constraints. Existing Void, crystal, encounter and reference tests also remain required.

`tests/moon_justiciar.rs` adds fixed arithmetic for the Moon weapons, full-set discovery and constraints, conditional spell/melee distributions, Justiciar per-hit reduction and typeless exclusion, and Torag's amulet defence. Eclipse's constant-time burn-stack calculation is checked against independent finite-state stationary enumeration for different probabilities, durations and caps. This is source/formula verification, not gameplay capture. `assets/moon-rules.json` imports strong-burn immunity IDs from the same pinned Wiki calculator NPC catalogue; spell icons come from the Wiki's corresponding spell PNGs.

`tests/eclipse_timing.rs` verifies the finite burn event model against fixed timelines and an independently enumerated probability tree. Immediate hits retain their accuracy/proc correlation, including accurate zeroes. Burn pulse damage uses the matching ranged NPC reduction on the combined stack hitsplat. Death stops pulses and shots, HP-window damage is capped, and cooldowns survive boundaries. Recommended Ranged comparisons use finite workloads on both sides when a usable Eclipse set is available; encounter switch comparisons do so whenever either side has burns. The old stationary loss-system figure is only a long-run reference, not finite-HP damage credited upfront.
